Our Story

A Cafe With a Soul

Ted Bitter and Drew Claxton turned an abandoned 1850s blacksmith shop into Peekskill's beloved community gathering place. Since opening in 2008, BeanRunner has become one of Westchester County's premier jazz venues — beloved by musicians and audiences alike.

Redesign Prototype by Claude · June 2026
🎨 Color System
Espresso brown (#1C0F0A) + warm amber (#C8963E) + cream (#F5EFE0). Rooted in the café's physical identity — coffee, wood, candlelight.
🔤 Typography
Playfair Display (serif, editorial) for headings → conveys heritage and sophistication. DM Sans (geometric sans) for body → clean and modern. DM Mono for labels/metadata → structured, café-menu feel.
🏠 Hero Section
Full-width, immersive dark hero with a 55% photo panel (placeholder here). Two clear CTAs replace the current zero-CTA homepage. Info strip replaces the hours JPEG image.
📋 Navigation
Consolidated from 8 items to 6. Removed duplicate "Menus" link and Wix "Log In" button. "Reserve a Table" becomes the persistent primary CTA in the nav.
🎵 Events Page
Structured weekly schedule cards + upcoming event cards replace the empty calendar widget. Inline "Reserve" buttons reduce friction vs. email-only flow.
📍 Contact Page
Google Map embed (placeholder) + hours as HTML text (not image) + proper contact form with labeled fields. All critical info above the fold.
⚡ Platform Note
Most Phase 1–2 changes can be done in Wix. Full visual rebrand (Phase 3) may warrant migration to Webflow or custom build for full control.
