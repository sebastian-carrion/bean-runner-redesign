Our Story

More than a cafe.
A place to belong.

BeanRunner has been a fixture in Peekskill since 2010 — a place where the espresso is serious, the food is made with care, and everyone walks in as a stranger and leaves as a regular. Named for the historic Peekskill running culture, we believe great coffee and great community run side by side.
